# Ideas pool A: random-seeded brainstorm

Scope: OpusLegends, a single-level 2D canvas rhythm-platformer. The music is an assumed riff-heavy track at ~150 BPM, so a beat is 400 ms, an 8th note 200 ms, a 16th note 100 ms and a 4/4 bar 1.6 s.

The whole point of this pass was to **pull ideas from outside the average**. Every idea below is tagged with the random source that produced it, e.g. `[src: Met 22226]`. Where two unrelated random sources collided on the same theme, I called it out, because collisions like that make the most distinctive material.

Scores are **O / F / Fe**: originality, fun potential and feasibility in 2D procedural canvas, each rated 1-5. ~~Struck-through~~ ideas are rejected, and each one has a one-line reason.

## 1. Heroes (10)

**H1. The Pyrrhicist**: `[src: pyrrhicist + Majaelrayo danzantes + Hoplite]`. **O4 / F5 / Fe5**
A long-legged dancer-warrior. Nearly the whole silhouette is **one huge round shield**: a perfect circle with a single bold emblem, which reads clearly at 24 px. A tall horsehair crest whips on every landing. Personality: showboating and joyous, and he treats combat as choreography.
- **Movement:** a springy, bouncing run where every footfall lands on an 8th note.
- **Attack:** a shield bash, or a shield **rim-spin** thrown like a discus that boomerangs back on the next downbeat.
- **Signature move, "Pyrrhic Double" (∪∪ then –):** two short dodge-hops on consecutive 8ths, then a long lunge-strike on the beat. Plato described the pyrrhic dance as dodge, then attack, and this move is that described literally.
- **Draw:** a circle, two thin legs, a crest bezier, and a motion-smear arc.

**H2. Swift, the Typesetting Racer**: `[src: PDR "Twilight of the Velocipede" + writmaker]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe4**
A 19th-century compositor with **sleeve garters, a green eyeshade visor and an ink-black apron**. She carries a **composing stick** (a long metal tray) as her weapon. Personality: a proud speed-demon from the working-class sport of dime-museum crowds.
- **Attack:** she flicks lead type-sorts like throwing knives, one per hi-hat.
- **Signature move, "JUSTIFY!":** on the crash cymbal she slams the full composing stick down. The line of letters she has collected locks into a solid slab with a *ka-CHUNK* and becomes the ground.
- **Draw:** the silhouette of the eyeshade and apron, plus glyphs rendered with `fillText`.

**H3. The Hollow Helm**: `[src: Met 22226 Gouffier close helmet]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
An **empty, bodiless close helmet** that hops along on a flowing ribbon-plume, with a **gold ΧΦ monogram** blazing on the brow. The visor is its mouth. It avoids the "generic knight" because there is no knight, only the armour, animated and hungry.
- **Signature move, "Visor Chomp":** the visor slams shut on the snare to swallow projectiles, then spits them back.
- **Idle:** it rings like a bell when struck, so hits literally *add* percussion.
- **Draw:** one strong shape (dome, beak visor and eye-slit) and a gradient steel sheen. It is very readable small.

**H4. The Botarga**: `[src: Majaelrayo festival]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe5**
The festival's striped trickster: a full-body **candy-stripe suit in red and green**, a floppy hood and a **ribbon pole** as tall as a house. The stripes **scroll at tempo** like a barber pole, so the character itself is a visible metronome.
- **Signature move, "Pole Vault on the One":** plant the pole on beat 1 and vault. The ribbons stream behind in a coloured trail whose shape *is* your rhythm history.
- **Attack:** a ribbon lash.
- **Draw:** stripe patterns clipped into a blob body, with ribbons drawn as verlet ropes.

**H5. ~~Kokila the Nightingale~~**: `[src: Kokila (1990) / Ilaiyaraaja]`. **O2 / F3 / Fe5**
A tiny songbird whose sung notes are projectiles, with an "Ascending Run" of jumps that rise in pitch.
> *Rejected: a cute bird hero is the mean. The ascending-only idea is stronger as a mechanic (M7).*

**H6. The Pollen-Crowned Honeyeater**: `[src: Eremophila genus]`. **O4 / F3 / Fe4**
A slim nectar bird with a **long curved bill and a crown that visibly fills with golden pollen** as you play. It **dips its head into downturned flowers on the hi-hat**, and each flower brushes pollen onto it, exactly as the real plants do.
- **Signature move, "Bloomburst":** on the drop, it shakes its crown and releases the stored pollen as a wave that blooms the whole screen.
- **Why:** the hero's appearance becomes a readable score meter.

**H7. Glass Charles**: `[src: PDR "The Glass Delusion" (Charles VI) + glareless]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe4**
A small crowned king who is (or believes he is) **made of glass**: a translucent faceted body with one matte, glareless heart visible inside. He is anxious but brave.
- **Movement:** every on-beat landing makes his body **ring a tuned wine-glass note**, so a perfect run plays a glass-harmonica countermelody. Off-beat landings put a visible crack in him (a health bar drawn as fractures).
- **Signature move, "Resonance":** he flicks his own chest on the crash and emits a shockwave ring that shatters enemies. Fragility is his weapon.
- **Draw:** faceted polygons, a gradient and white edge lines.

**H8. The Writmaker**: `[src: writmaker + Commons random bureaucratic letter]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
A tiny court clerk dwarfed by a **giant brass seal-stamp** carried like a sledgehammer. He wears a quill behind his ear, and a scroll trails behind him like a cape.
- **Signature move, "SERVED!":** a kick-drum overhead stamp that leaves a **red wax-seal splat** on enemies and the ground. The seals stay behind as a visible record of your on-beat hits across the level.
- **Personality:** officious, deadpan and unstoppable.

**H9. Wheellock Wren**: `[src: Met 22376 wheellock carbine]`. **O4 / F3 / Fe4**
A clockwork girl with a **big serrated steel wheel on her back** and a spanner key. Hi-hat 16ths **wind the wheel** (click-click-click, with the ratchet visible), and releasing on the snare **grinds the wheel on pyrite** in a fan of orange sparks that kills anything in front.
- **Signature move:** a spark fan.
- Overlaps somewhat with H3's percussion idea, but the wind/release verb is unique.

**H10. ~~The Vegetable Lamb~~**: `[src: PDR essay list, "Animal, Vegetable, Lamb"]`. **O5 / F2 / Fe4**
A lamb that grows on a stalk and grazes in a circle around its stem.
> *Rejected: being tethered to a stem fights the core "sprint right" fantasy. It might work as an enemy or collectible instead.*

## 2. World / theme concepts (10)

**W1. Pueblos Negros: the Slate Village Festival**: `[src: Majaelrayo]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe5**
A mountain village built entirely of **black slate**, at dusk on festival night.
- **Palette:** slate blue-black, warm pine-wood frames, **ember-orange windows** and hot **magenta, yellow and turquoise festival ribbons** strung between the eaves. The contrast is superb for a canvas game.
- **Parallax:**
  - the far Sierra de Ayllón peaks
  - mid-ground terraced slate roofs
  - near bunting and ribbon lines
  - foreground flower-mitre silhouettes of the crowd
- **How the world plays the music:** the overlapping slate roof tiles are a giant **marimba**, and your footfalls clack pitched notes. The drum director on the church tower beats the kick. Ribbons snap taut on the snare.

**W2. The Stack Coast**: `[src: Forillon + Stack (geology)]`. **O4 / F5 / Fe5**
Chalk-white cliffs fall into a teal sea, and the coast is going through the **cave, arch, stack and stump** cycle in fast-forward.
- **Palette:** bone white, teal, coral sunset and black seabird silhouettes.
- **Parallax:**
  - distant whale spouts
  - a mid-row of stacks
  - arches the player runs over
  - foreground surf spray
- **Music:** waves booming in sea caves are the **kick**, clouds of nesting birds chatter the **hi-hat**, and a lighthouse beam sweeps once per bar. Strong **vertical** potential (seed word) for climbing a Ball's Pyramid-style spire.

**W3. The Theodrama Stage**: `[src: theodrama + Kyogen overvest]`. **O3 / F4 / Fe5**
The level is a colossal **theatre stage**. Parallax layers are **painted flats on ropes** that fly in and out with the song sections, and you can see the counterweights and stagehands in the wings. The Kyogen pattern (bamboo and webs) is painted on the backdrops.
- **Music:** flats drop in on downbeats, spotlights snap on accents, and the curtain is the song's structure (intro = curtain rise, break = blackout).
- **O3** because "stage worlds" exist in other games. It is still very strong as a **framing device** for another theme.

**W4. Desert Foxglove Dunes at Blue Hour**: `[src: Eremophila platythamnos]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
Red-sand dunes under a **glareless** indigo sky, dotted with shrubs that **glisten with sticky resin**. Their purple-and-white tube flowers **open on the snare**. Emus bob along the ridge line, and dunes **breathe** (a sine-wave height change) on the bass riff.
- **Palette:** rust, violet, white and a single gold for pollen.

**W5. The Signal Line**: `[src: Patent US150150 + Commons Marunouchi Line]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe4**
A night-time telegraph railway. **Signal gantries** straddle the line every "mile" (every bar), and each has a row of **brass keys**. The train's roof-fingers strike the keys, and **the strikes are the beat**, the rhythm literally encoded in the infrastructure as in the 1874 patent. Telegraph wires overhead ripple with the riff like a waveform. Later the line **dives underground and bursts back out over a river**, Marunouchi-style.
- **Palette:** ink blue, sodium amber, signal red and green.

**W6. The Composing Room and Dime Museum**: `[src: PDR Typesetting races]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe4**
You run across **giant type cases**: the compartments are pits, and the lead sorts are platforms. Galleys slide around like trams. Above, a **dime-museum balcony** of silhouetted crowds cheers the race, and a huge chalkboard ticks off "ems per hour".
- **Palette:** ink black, newsprint cream and vermilion proof-marks.
- **Music:** letter-stamps and press strikes are percussion, and the chorus lyric is *typeset live* as the ground (see M9).

**W7. Kremenets Hill of the Stone Babas**: `[src: Izium / stone babas]`. **O4 / F3 / Fe5**
A windswept steppe hill of **ochre grass** under a **lilac sky**, with rows of squat stone idols holding bowls. The grass waves in **tempo-synced ripples** (the riff drawn as wind), and the idols hum the vocal hook in low drones.
- Moody. Better as a **section** or set-piece (S6) than as the whole level.

**W8. The Glass Court**: `[src: Glass Delusion + glareless]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe3**
A palace where everything is glass: chandeliers, stairs and courtiers. Everything **rings** when struck (pitched to the song's key), and every crash cymbal splinters something.
- **Art challenge:** refraction, faked with layered alpha gradients and edge highlights. It must stay **frosted and glareless** or it becomes visual noise.
- **Fe3** for that reason.

**W9. ~~Bierstadt's Golden Valley~~**: `[src: Met 10150 Merced River]`. **O2 / F3 / Fe5**
Luminous haze, granite walls, and waterfalls as sustained riff notes.
> *Rejected: gorgeous, but "pretty sunset valley" is exactly the mean. The god-ray pulse can be borrowed as a lighting trick anywhere.*

**W10. The Woven World**: `[src: Met 62222 bamboo basket + Met 44452 Kyogen spiderweb vest]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe5**
The world is **woven from bamboo lattice**: basket-weave hills, lattice bridges and giant hanging baskets as platforms. **Spiderwebs are strung between the bamboo like guitar strings**, and the **riff is literally plucked on them**. The strands vibrate visibly (sine displacement) on each note.
- **Palette:** straw gold, indigo dye and chalk-white web lines, which gives very bold graphic contrast.
- **This is the most direct "the environment is the instrument" concept in the list: the riff has a physical object.**

## 3. Beat-synced mechanics and obstacles (20)

Notation: K = kick, S = snare, H = hi-hat, R = main guitar riff, V = vocal hook, X = crash, D = drop, B = break/stop.

**M1. Pyrrhic Double (∪∪ –)**: `[src: pyrrhicist]`. **O4 / F5 / Fe5**
- **Do:** tap twice on two consecutive 8ths (two short dodge-hops), then hold/strike on the beat.
- **Music:** maps onto the **R pickup notes** into a **K/S hit**, the da-da-DUM that almost every riff has.
- **Fun:** it turns a single "jump on beat" into a *phrase*, which feels like dancing.
- **Telegraph:** the metrical marks ∪ ∪ – are chalked on the ground ahead, doubling as notation (see X4).

**M2. Sikerness Ground**: `[src: sikerness]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe5**
- **Do:** the ground ahead is a *dashed, uncertain outline*. Every on-beat footfall **solidifies the next tile** (it becomes *sicker*, secure) with a satisfying ink-fill. Off-beat steps leave a tile sketchy, and it crumbles after one bar.
- **Music:** **K** (every beat), with denser 8th-note footings when the R double-times.
- **Fun:** you *write your own floor* in time. It is a precision layer that doesn't need extra buttons.
- **Telegraph:** dashed tiles pulse on the beat, and the fill animation shows the timing grade.

**M3. Web Strings**: `[src: Met 44452 Kyogen webs]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe5**
- **Do:** spider-silk strands cross the path at various heights. Running or punching through a strand **plucks a riff note**. Some strands are thick "wound" strings that must be **punched on exactly the R note** or they bounce you back like a trampoline.
- **Music:** the **R**, note for note. The player is *playing the guitar line* by moving through space.
- **Fun:** by far the most direct "you are performing the riff" mechanic.
- **Telegraph:** each strand vibrates slightly *one beat before* its note, like a string about to be picked.

**M4. Las Fajas: Ribbon Swing**: `[src: Majaelrayo "Las Fajas"]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe4**
- **Do:** grab the ribbon hanging from a festival pole and **swing around it** (a circular arc). Release on the **X** to fling off. Two poles in a row need a mid-swing hand-off on **S**.
- **Music:** one revolution = one bar, so the release window is the downbeat.
- **Fun:** momentum plus rhythm, and the ribbons wrap and unwind visibly as a timer.
- **Telegraph:** the ribbon colour cycles through 4 colours per revolution, and release on magenta.

**M5. Signal Gantry Code**: `[src: Patent US150150]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe5**
- **Do:** overhead gantries with rows of brass keys. Jump-strike the keys in the pattern of the R phrase ("two, pause, three"). Completing a gantry **transmits your code**, so the next station's signal flips green and the barrier lifts.
- **Music:** R phrasing, or a guitar stab pattern.
- **Fun:** a call-and-response puzzle at full speed, where each gantry is a musical phrase.
- **Telegraph:** the key caps light up in sequence during the bar *before* (call), then you answer (response).

**M6. Wet Powder Squall**: `[src: Battle of Honey Springs]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe4**
- **Do:** a battery of cannons fires cannonballs on **K**. Hit a thunder-drum or cloud-bellows on **S** to drag a **rain squall** over them. The soaked cannons **misfire comically**, giving a *pfft* and a smoke puff instead of a shot.
- **Music:** the misfire *pfft* is sequenced to land on the **H** off-beats, so the arrangement shifts from threatening to playful.
- **Fun:** you don't dodge the obstacle, you *mute* it. The player changes the mix.
- **Telegraph:** fuses fizz for exactly 2 beats before firing.

**M7. The Ascending-Only Stair**: `[src: Ilaiyaraaja's ascending-only song]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
- **Do:** platforms appear one per note of a rising melodic run, each higher than the last and each **tuned a step higher**. They exist only while their note sounds. Miss one and the melody "falls" back down, collapsing the stair to where you are.
- **Music:** a **rising lead fill** or vocal run (every rock song has one before a chorus).
- **Fun:** you climb the pitch itself, and it builds tension into the chorus beautifully.
- **Telegraph:** ghost platforms appear half a beat early, and pitch is shown by colour (the hue rises with the note).

**M8. Stamp and Serve**: `[src: writmaker]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
- **Do:** unsealed scrolls (enemies or blockers) tumble towards you. Slam-stamp each on **K**. Every perfect stamp leaves a wax-seal splat, and five seals in a row **chain into a ribbon** that becomes a zipline.
- **Music:** K, with 8th-note doubles on the "four-on-the-floor" bridge.
- **Fun:** chunky, tactile hits that leave visible proof of accuracy on the world.
- **Telegraph:** a scroll unrolls a "SIGN HERE" line one beat before its impact spot.

**M9. Typeset the Chorus**: `[src: PDR Typesetting races]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe4**
- **Do:** during the vocal hook, lead letter-sorts fly out of the type case in lyric order. Punch or catch each on its syllable, and they snap into your composing stick. On the **X** at the end of the line, "JUSTIFY" locks them into a **solid bridge of letters** that you run across over a chasm.
- **Music:** **V** syllables. The song's lyrics literally become the level geometry.
- **Fun:** it is a karaoke-platformer hybrid, and people will notice the words.
- **Telegraph:** each letter glows on its syllable, and the bridge shows ghost outlines.

**M10. Feigned Death**: `[src: Tokhtamysh / Edigu's fake death]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe5**
- **Do:** some enemies collapse theatrically when you hit them **off-beat**, but they're faking. Their toe still taps the tempo. The real kill is on the next **downbeat**.
- **Music:** teaches downbeat discipline, and punishes mashing in a funny, readable way.
- **Fun:** a joke that teaches.
- **Telegraph:** a real death gives a big wax splat and "burst", while a fake death gives a comic "X_X" face and a still-tapping foot.

**M11. Arch Collapse Sprint**: `[src: Stack (geology)]`. **O4 / F5 / Fe5**
- **Do:** a sea arch spans a chasm, and each **S** knocks out a chunk of its keystone. You must cross in 2 bars. On the **X** the arch falls, and the half you're standing on becomes a *stack*, which you leap off.
- **Music:** S counts down the crossing, and the **drum fill** before the X is the "run!" cue.
- **Fun:** a panic moment that is readable and fair.
- **Telegraph:** crack lines spread one per snare.

**M12. Visor Chomp Parry**: `[src: Met 22226]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
- **Do:** projectiles arrive on **H** 8ths. Close the visor or shield on each to *eat* it, then spit the stored ones back on **S**.
- **Music:** H (eat) to S (return) is the classic rock backbeat, so it feels like drumming.
- **Fun:** absorb-and-release, and the counter-attack sounds like your own snare hit.
- **Telegraph:** projectiles travel along beat-spaced dotted tracks.

**M13. Wheellock Wind-Up**: `[src: Met 22376]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe4**
- **Do:** hold during a hi-hat 16th run to wind a spring. The ratchet visibly clicks once per 16th. Release on the S to fire a spark fan. Releasing late jams the wheel with a *clunk* and a one-beat stall.
- **Music:** H 16ths as the build, S as the release.
- **Fun:** the tension of a charge shot, locked to the groove.
- **Telegraph:** a ratchet ring with 16 teeth, and release when the ring closes.

**M14. ~~Blowpipe Breath~~**: `[src: Met 30150]`. **O3 / F3 / Fe5**
Hold your breath over a sustained vocal note, and release to fire a dart.
> *Rejected: a duplicate hold-and-release verb (M13 does it better). Sustained vocal notes are also rare in a riff-heavy track.*

**M15. Glass Landing**: `[src: Glass Delusion]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe5**
- **Do:** land on beat and your landing *rings a pitched note*. Land off-beat and you get a crack (that is the damage system). A perfect section plays a full glass-harmonica countermelody over the song.
- **Music:** K/S landings, with pitch drawn from the song's chord at that moment.
- **Fun:** a precision layer that makes the player *add music* instead of only hearing it.
- **Telegraph:** a landing-shadow ring shrinks to the beat.

**M16. Pollen Brush and the Bloom Drop**: `[src: Eremophila pollination]`. **O4 / F5 / Fe5**
- **Do:** flowers with **downturned lobes** hang from above. Duck or dip into each on **H** to collect pollen on your head. On the **D**, release it and the entire world blooms in a wave travelling at tempo.
- **Music:** H collection, then the D payoff. It is a whole section-long build and release.
- **Fun:** delayed gratification that visibly scales with your skill.
- **Telegraph:** flowers sway on the offbeat, dip in on the beat.

**M17. Flying Flats**: `[src: theodrama stage]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
- **Do:** painted scenery flats fly down from the rigging on **K** and up on the offbeat. They are platforms only while down, so you hop flat-to-flat as they alternate like pistons.
- **Music:** K down, H up. The whole screen breathes with the groove.
- **Fun:** pure groove platforming, and it looks gorgeous as silhouette cut-outs.
- **Telegraph:** a stagehand's cue whistle (a real theatre practice) and the counterweight dropping in the wings a beat early.

**M18. Sword Corridor (Ezpatadantza)**: `[src: Basque dance + Majaelrayo Las Espadas]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe5**
- **Do:** two rows of festival dancers raise swords into an arch corridor. The swords rise and fall in a **ripple wave** down the line. Slide under the dips on **S**, and hop the rising blades on **K**.
- **Music:** the ripple travels at exactly 1 dancer per 8th note, so the corridor is a visible waveform of the R.
- **Fun:** you run *through a dance*, and the dancers cheer at the end (see S3).
- **Telegraph:** the wave is visible from far off, and it is predictable.

**M19. Stone Gaze Freeze**: `[src: Izium stone babas]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
- **Do:** on the song's **B** (stop), the stone idols **turn their heads to face you**. Freeze exactly on the stop and hold through the silence. Moving means they "sing" you backwards.
- **Music:** built entirely on the **break**, the silent beat that every great riff song has.
- **Fun:** stillness as the challenge, the one moment the player must *not* act.
- **Telegraph:** heads begin to grind around on the last 2 beats before the stop.

**M20. ~~Othismos Shove~~**: `[src: Hoplite]`. **O4 / F2 / Fe5**
Tap on the beat to push a phalanx back, like a tug-of-war.
> *Rejected: mashing works against rhythm precision, and a stalemate breaks the sprint-right flow. At most, keep it as a 1-bar cameo.*

## 4. Enemies and creatures (10)

**E1. Stone Babas**: `[src: Izium]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
Squat idols holding bowls, with carved moustaches and a gradient patina.
- **On beat:** they bob a millimetre per K, like weighty head-nods.
- **On the break:** they turn to stare (M19).
- **When hit:** they chip, and the chips fall as collectibles.

**E2. Flehmen Goats**: `[src: Flehmen response]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe5**
Scruffy goats that **sniff the air, curl their lip and hold a laughing grimace for exactly one bar**. That grimace is the telegraph, and they headbutt on the next downbeat.
- **Why:** it is funny, instantly readable and grounded in real animal behaviour.
- **Counter:** hit them mid-grimace (it's an open window) for a bonus "rude!" pop.

**E3. The Flag-Wavers**: `[src: Siege of Moscow, the fake-peace deception]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
Gate guards who wave a **white flag on the offbeat**, then flip it into a **spear on the beat**. The player learns to read the flip. They pair with M10 (feigned death) as a "trickery" enemy family.

**E4. Web-Weaver Spiders**: `[src: Met 44452 Kyogen vest]`. **O4 / F5 / Fe5**
Long-legged, graphic spiders drawn Kyogen-style. They **pluck the web strings to play the riff**, and each pluck flings a dewdrop bullet on the note.
- **Punching one** steals its web, and for 1 bar the riff notes come from *your* hits (a player-driven solo moment).

**E5. Damp Powder Gunners**: `[src: Honey Springs]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe4**
Cannoneers in oilskin hats who fire on K. Under rain (M6) they **misfire**, look confused, and fire again on the next beat, still wet. They are harmless comic relief once soaked.

**E6. Printer's Devils**: `[src: Typesetting races; "printer's devil" is the historical name for a print-shop apprentice]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
Ink-black imps with inky hand-prints. On the **offbeats** they dart in and **steal letters from your composing stick**, and you must swat them back on the beat. They make M9 a two-way fight.

**E7. Emu Stampede**: `[src: Eremophila / emus]`. **O3 / F4 / Fe5**
A mob of emus runs alongside you, **head-bobbing on the H**. They are not really enemies: jump on their backs on the beat to get a bounce-chain, or mistime and get kicked.
- They are a good **mount-ish ally** for pacing variety.

**E8. ~~Stack Gannets~~**: `[src: Forillon seabirds]`. **O2 / F3 / Fe5**
Seabirds that dive-bomb on the snare.
> *Rejected: a generic dive-bomb bird (the mean). Seabirds are better as hi-hat ambience in W2.*

**E9. Glare Heralds**: `[src: glareless + Gouffier's polished steel]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe4**
Armoured heralds carrying **mirror-polished shields**. Once per bar they **swing a sunbeam of glare** across the screen, whitening everything it hits.
- **Counter:** the hero's **matte, glareless** shield (or ducking behind scenery on the beat) absorbs it.
- **Accessibility:** the glare must be a **bold shape**, a clear wedge beam and not a full-screen flash (see X1).

**E10. Pigeonhole Clerks**: `[src: writmaker + Commons bureaucratic letter]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
A wall of pigeonholes, from which mole-like clerks pop out on the **S** and stamp **"DENIED"** planks that block the path. You counter-stamp them on the next K to flip the plank into a "APPROVED" step. It is rhythmic whack-a-mole that builds the route.

## 5. Set-pieces and climax moments (8)

**S1. The Signal Line Finale**: `[src: Patent US150150 + Marunouchi Line]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe4**
You land on the roof of a night train, and **every gantry you pass is a bar of the song**, with your jumps striking the keys (M5). On the pre-chorus the train **dives into a tunnel**: pitch black, only the signal lamps strobing the H. On the **drop** it **bursts out over a river bridge** into a sky of fireworks, the camera pulls wide, and the whole train becomes a lit-up silhouette. This is the moment people will tell their friends about.

**S2. The Arch Falls**: `[src: Stack (geology) + vertical]`. **O4 / F5 / Fe5**
A mile of sea arches collapses **like dominoes to the drum fill**. Each falling arch becomes a stack, and you vault stack to stack as they topple, with a **vertical** climb up the last and tallest one (a Ball's Pyramid homage). Right on the crash, **a whale breaches** below, and you ride its splash to the next section.

**S3. The Procession**: `[src: Majaelrayo danzantes + Basque dance]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe4**
You run into the festival procession. First you get the **sword corridor** (M18). Then, if you hit every beat, the eight flower-crowned dancers **catch you and lift you horizontally** (the Basque "fallen warrior" lift) and **pass you over the crowd** during the drum roll. Finally they **launch you** onto the slate rooftops, which become a marimba (W1). Failing is also fun: you get dumped comically in the crowd.

**S4. The Lyric Bridge**: `[src: PDR Typesetting races]`. **O5 / F5 / Fe4**
During the final chorus, a chasm opens, and you build the bridge out of the **lyric itself** (M9) while the dime-museum balcony roars and a giant chalkboard counts "EMS PER HOUR" towards a record. On the last word, JUSTIFY slams, the bridge locks, confetti made of type sorts erupts, and a headline is printed across the sky with your score.

**S5. ~~The Deus ex Machina Face~~**: `[src: theodrama]`. **O2 / F3 / Fe4**
A giant god-mask is lowered on a crane and lip-syncs the vocal while you run across it.
> *Rejected: the giant singing face is a Rayman/Castle Rock-adjacent cliché. The crane idea lives on in M17.*

**S6. The Stone Choir**: `[src: Izium stone babas]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
You crest a hilltop of **forty stone idols**, and the song **stops dead** (B). In the silence, every head grinds round to face you (M19). You must hold still for 2 beats. Then they **all sing the hook** at once, the hill lights gold from beneath, and the song crashes back in, louder. It works because silence is the most powerful beat in the song.

**S7. The Glass Court Shatters**: `[src: Glass Delusion + glareless]`. **O4 / F5 / Fe4**
The climax: every surface in the glass palace rings the melody as you race through it. On the final crash the **entire world shatters**, and time slows to 25%. Shards drift as collectible confetti, you have 1 bar to grab them in slow motion, and on the next downbeat everything **snaps back together**. The illusion *heals* (Charles VI believed himself glass, and the ending is that he isn't).

**S8. The Bloom Drop**: `[src: Eremophila]`. **O4 / F5 / Fe5**
The whole pre-drop section is spent collecting pollen (M16). On the **drop**, you release it, and a **wave of purple-and-white flowers erupts across the desert at exactly tempo speed**, one dune per beat, rolling to the horizon while emus scatter. The size of the bloom is *your score*, made visible.

## 6. Wild cards (5)

**X1. Glareless Design Pledge**: `[src: glareless]`. **O4 / F4 / Fe5**
Make "no flashes" a *visual identity*, not an afterthought. All beat feedback comes through **shape, scale and motion**: squash, pop and ink-fill, never full-screen white strobes. The result is photosensitivity-safe, distinct from the flashing rhythm-game norm, and more readable. The one allowed "glare" is diegetic, the Glare Heralds' beam (E9), and even that is a wedge shape.

**X2. The Level is a Writ**: `[src: writmaker]`. **O4 / F3 / Fe5**
The results screen is an **illuminated legal writ**. The level's path is drawn as calligraphy, and every perfect hit is a red wax seal along it, which makes it a shareable image of your run. Mostly flavour, but cheap and charming.

**X3. ~~Donor's Eyes Ghost~~**: `[src: Kokila, the cornea donor's memories]`. **O3 / F3 / Fe5**
The hero "remembers" a previous run as a ghost.
> *Rejected: replay ghosts are standard, and the eerie framing clashes with the upbeat tone.*

**X4. Scansion Notation instead of a Note Highway**: `[src: pyrrhicist, metrical feet]`. **O5 / F4 / Fe5**
Instead of arrows or lanes, upcoming rhythm is marked **on the world** in **poetic metre**: ∪ = short (8th), – = long (quarter), and | = bar line. These are chalked on walls, sewn into ribbons and carved into stones. It is a unique, diegetic, learnable notation that doubles as art, and it fits a hero who is literally a metre-dancer.

**X5. Your Train Number**: `[src: Patent US150150]`. **O5 / F3 / Fe4**
In the 1874 patent, the strike pattern *encodes the train's number*. Likewise, each run produces a **rhythm signature**: which optional hits you took, turned into a short code (e.g. "23-7-41"). Share the code, and a friend races your ghost *as a train on the parallel track* in the Signal Line finale.
